Events During the Week of Welcome (WOW)

1st Day

On our first day of WOW, we felt nervous because everything was new to us. However, as the event went on, we started to feel more comfortable and enjoyed every moment.

2nd Day

On our second day of WOW, we were already more comfortable with our surroundings. We were less nervous compared to the first day and started to enjoy the activities more. We spent time with our classmates, talked with new people, and had fun together. It was a simple but enjoyable day for us.

3rd Day

On our third day of WOW, we became more confident and comfortable participating in the different activities. We enjoyed every moment with our classmates and friends. We also learned new things and experienced activities that made our day more memorable. We were happy because we were able to enjoy the event without feeling too nervous anymore.

4th Day

On our fourth day of WOW, we were already familiar with the activities and the environment. We participated more actively and enjoyed spending time with everyone. We also took pictures, laughed together, and created more memories. The day was filled with fun experiences that we will remember.

5th Day

On our fifth and final day of WOW, we felt happy but also a little sad because the event was coming to an end. Looking back, we realized how much we enjoyed the whole week. We were thankful for the new experiences, friendships, and memories we made. The WOW event became a memorable part of our school experience.

The Best Part

YOHOOOOOOO!

The best part of WOW? Knowing that the next week is the start of classes! HAHAHA! After all the fun and excitement, it’s finally time to say goodbye to free time and welcome the stress, assignments, and endless deadlines. What a wonderful way to end the week! 😭📚
